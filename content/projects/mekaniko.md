## Context

Mekaniko+ is an e-commerce platform for construction material suppliers and buyers (in this case consultant), built while I worked at Ardora Cipta Kreasi. I built both halves of it: a Go backend and a Laravel frontend, both speakingg through gRPC.

Big suppliers (principals) needed their own portal to manage their catalog and pricing, and building consultant needed to purchase against those supplier-specific catalogs. They can also become a reseller of a specific brand, becoming a "down-line" in a sense
