## The longer version

Moneta started as a point-of-sale system for FnB and tourism businesses, built with Laravel and Go while I worked at Artamaya Makmur Abadi. It offers extensive features for FnB businesses (inventory, kitchen order sync, financial breakdown, QR cart and checkout system) and tourism businesses.

## What i learned

This is the first time i use both gRPC and laravel on a production scale project. Turns out, gRPC is a fascinating peace of engineering and really has a lot of use cases.
