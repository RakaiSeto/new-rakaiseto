## Context

Lyrink is a companion app for Youtube Music consisted of both an Android App and a Linux widget (currently supporting Dank Material Shell and KDE Plasma). The purpose is to show the lyric of the currently playing song in your android device to the widget in your linux app line per line. Other than that, you can also control the playback as with play, pause, skip, previous, and the elapsed time. The widget itself can also detected the media you played in your local device.
