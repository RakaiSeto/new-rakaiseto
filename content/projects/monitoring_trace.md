## Context

Since my place of internship already have an app to track employee's activity, i proposed to make a helper app to generate our required logbook by consuming the internal app's API then parsing the data to a Typst template. The output is PDF that are ready to be signed by our supervisor. In the end, the feature got absorbed as a mini app to the internal app.

## What i learned

Generating, preparing, and using Typst template for real world app.
